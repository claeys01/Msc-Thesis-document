\chapter{Validation}

Because the goal of this framework is too accelerate a running WaterLily simulation, this chapter will highlight the influence of various parameters of $\mathcal{P}$ that significantly impact the computational cost of training the pipeline $\mathcal{P}$. Following the outline of chapter \ref{chap: Methodology}, this chapter will discuss relevant parameters of the autoencoder, mainly the influence of the amount of training epochs, and rank the variation of these parameters by reconstruction quality. Additionally the best model achieved by hyperparameter tuning will be highlighted to serve as a benchmark for following architecture variations. Following this the neural ODE will be discussed, where the influence of the amount of training iterations, the group size of the multiple shooting optimisation and the best architecture will be highlighted.
